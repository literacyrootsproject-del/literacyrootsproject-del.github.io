Literacy Begins at Home

A child's first classroom is the home. Their first teachers are the people who love and care for them. Long before kindergarten, everyday experiences—talking, reading, singing, playing, and connecting—help build the language, relationships, and early literacy skills children need to succeed.

But not every family has equal access to the resources and support that make these experiences possible. Financial hardship, housing instability, and other challenges can create barriers to early learning.

The Literacy Roots Project works to remove those barriers. By providing free early literacy education, practical parenting tools, and connections to community resources, we help families create nurturing environments where children can learn, grow, and thrive.

Why We Support the Whole Family

Children don't learn in isolation. Their development is shaped by the relationships, experiences, and environments surrounding them.

That's why The Literacy Roots Project takes a two-generation approach—supporting children's early learning while strengthening the knowledge, skills, and confidence of the adults who care for them.

Because when families have the tools and support to thrive, children have a stronger foundation for success.

Our Programs

Early Literacy Workshops

Interactive workshops for expectant parents and caregivers of children ages 0–3, offering evidence-based strategies to nurture early language and literacy development through everyday activities, shared reading, and responsive interactions that lay the foundation for lifelong learning.

Parents & Caregivers Program

A free, seven-week program empowering expectant parents and caregivers of young children with practical knowledge of child development, positive discipline, early literacy, and family well-being. Participants build parenting confidence, strengthen family relationships, and connect with essential community resources.

Summer Early Literacy Enrichment Program

A free, five-week summer program for children ages 4–6, combining individualized literacy instruction, storytelling, creative exploration, and social-emotional learning. Through engaging, developmentally appropriate activities, children build foundational reading skills, confidence, curiosity, and readiness for success in school.

Curiosity Club

In partnership with The RED Bookshelf, Curiosity Club connects children with books selected around their unique interests. Personalized book bundles encourage independent exploration, spark imagination, strengthen reading motivation, and help children build home libraries that inspire lifelong curiosity.

Community Outreach & Collaboration

Building strong community partnerships that connect families to resources, support services, and local opportunities that promote family well-being, strengthen stability, and reduce barriers--ultimately allowing caregivers to focus on their family's success.

Our Mission

The Literacy Roots Project strengthens children, families, and communities through early literacy education, parent empowerment, and family-centered support.

Through two-generation learning initiatives, we support children's early development while equipping parents and caregivers with the knowledge, skills, confidence, and resources to foster learning at home, advocate for their children, and build healthy, nurturing family environments.

Our mission is to expand educational opportunity, strengthen family stability, and help break intergenerational cycles of poverty—beginning with the earliest years of life.
